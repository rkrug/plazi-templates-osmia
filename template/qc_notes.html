• Most errors are normal editorial work in GoldenGATE, not problems of the styles: all 76 articles still lack a Zenodo identifier and links for their figures (they come with the upload), references have journal names or years to correct, and taxon names lack authorities or higher ranks.
  • Tables cause 1,240 of the 1,402 blockers (fragmented and mingled cells and rows in 23 PDFs). GoldenGATE detects table grids itself; editors correct them in the table editor.
  • Three error groups come from the styles and can be fixed in a version 4:
    • Missing publication date in 30 PDFs: the 2007-2012 and 2016-2020 styles have no rule for it, although the month is printed (running head “Janvier 2007”, “Publication: juillet 2020”).
    • Missed captions (121 in 31 PDFs, all styles): the caption patterns know only “Figure <n>”; the PDFs also use “Map”, “Figures”, “Table I.” and “Table S1”, and some “Figure <n>.” captions are missed for another reason that needs a look.
    • Broken structure (454, mostly in five 2021- PDFs): headings such as “REFERENCES” are not recognised, so the text after them lies outside any section.
  • Paragraph start/end errors in 75 PDFs are partly editorial work and partly the licence block of the 2021- style (“… License” taken as running text).
  • The same two non-articles as in the template check could not be converted (a scanned PDF, an unreadable image).
