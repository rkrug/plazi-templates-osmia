The one left, Osmia_7_W3090985975, prints no date at all.

In return it lists more captions it cannot match to a figure (+23) or that are never cited (+14), and more figure citations to link (+35): editorial work in GoldenGATE. The remaining missed captions sit beside or inside figures, or are numbered with Roman numerals.
