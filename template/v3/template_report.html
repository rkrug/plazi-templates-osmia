Reading

  • Recognition is confirmed. GoldenGATE assigns every PDF the same style as the R checker: 30 / 14 / 29 / 3 for the four styles. GoldenGATE also tested all cached Plazi styles, and none of them claimed an Osmia PDF.
  • Version 3 of Osmia.2016-2020 fixes the authors of volumes 6–8. In version 2, GoldenGATE kept only the first author in 9 of the 14 PDFs. For a multi-value field it takes one value per match of the context pattern, and the old context matched the whole author line. The new context matches each author separately, chained from the opening “par”. GoldenGATE now extracts every author OpenAlex counts (up to 24), and nothing from the abstracts.
  • GoldenGATE writes some surnames with particles in the wrong order: “Féon, Violette Le” for Violette LE FÉON, “Divelec, Romain Le”, “J.Vereecken, Nicolas”. That comes from GoldenGATE's name splitting, not from the styles; correct such names in GoldenGATE when the articles are processed.
  • GoldenGATE does better than the R checker in 3 PDFs. It finds DOI, volume and pagination where the R checker found none (Osmia_4_W3091727783, Osmia_7_W3090985975), and reads “Jean-Luc Renneson” in full. In Osmia_8_W3216943973 the two only differ in a space (“LE DIVELEC” / “LEDIVELEC”).
  • The OpenAlex differences are on the OpenAlex side. Some titles are worded differently or misspelt (“Éditorial :” prefix, “Fabricus”, “Compte-rendu”). Osmia_NA_W7166478142 is attached to the wrong OpenAlex work: its DOI, title and pages are those of the next article.
  • Two non-articles could not be read by GoldenGATE: a scanned PDF processed as born-digital (DT=D), and a full issue with an image GoldenGATE could not read back. Neither affects the styles.
