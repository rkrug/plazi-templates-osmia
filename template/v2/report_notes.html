The R checker and OpenAlex both have 2–24 authors there. The author rule of that style works differently in GoldenGATE and needs fixing before the style goes into production.
  • GoldenGATE does better than the R checker in 2 PDFs. It finds DOI, volume and pagination where the R checker found none (Osmia_4_W3091727783, Osmia_7_W3090985975), and it reads “Jean-Luc Renneson” where the R checker reads only “Luc”.
